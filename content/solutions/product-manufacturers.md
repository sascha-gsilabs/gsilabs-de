    items:
      - q: How long does it take to build a 3D configurator?
        a: >-
          It depends on the complexity of your product range. A single product line with
          clear options can be live in 6 to 8 weeks. More complex portfolios with
          interdependencies between dimensions, materials, and accessories take 10 to 14
          weeks. We always start with your highest volume products first.
      - q: Do we need to maintain the BIM objects ourselves?
        a: >-
          No. The BIM objects are connected to your product data. When you update a
          product specification, the BIM objects update automatically. There is no manual
          file management or version control on your side.
      - q: How is the AI assistant trained?
        a: >-
          We train it exclusively on your technical documentation, product catalogs,
          installation guides, and FAQs. It does not use generic construction knowledge.
          Before it goes live, your technical team reviews and approves the responses to
          make sure they are accurate.
      - q: Can the configurator connect to our ERP or pricing system?
        a: >-
          Yes. We build integrations with common ERP systems so the configurator can pull
          live pricing, stock availability, and lead times. Customers see real data, not
          outdated catalog prices.
      - q: What if a customer configures something that is not producible?
        a: >-
          The configurator enforces your production rules in real time. Invalid
          combinations are blocked before the customer can submit them. This means your
          sales team only receives valid, producible configurations.
      - q: How do we measure the impact?
        a: >-
          We provide dashboards that track configuration completions, BIM downloads, AI
          assistant interactions, and specification document generation. You can see
          exactly how many specifications your digital tools generate compared to
          traditional channels.
