  - type: faq
    title: Questions design offices ask us
    note: If you have further questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
    cta: { label: Contact us, href: /get-started }
    items:
      - q: Which BIM platforms do you support?
        a: >-
          We build plugins for Revit, Tekla Structures, Allplan, and Archicad. If your
          office uses a different authoring tool, let us know. The approach is the same
          regardless of platform: we analyze your workflows first and then build
          directly into the environment your team already uses.
      - q: How long does it take to develop a custom plugin?
        a: >-
          Most plugins go from first workshop to a working version in 6 to 10 weeks.
          Simpler automations like standardized element placement can be ready in 4
          weeks. More complex workflows involving multiple standards or cross platform
          logic may take up to 12 weeks. We always deliver a testable version early so
          your team can provide feedback before the final release.
      - q: Will the plugin work with our existing templates and standards?
        a: >-
          Yes. That is the entire point. Every plugin is built around your specific
          templates, naming conventions, layer structures, and drawing standards. We do
          not ship generic tools. We start by reviewing your existing project setup and
          make sure the automation produces output that is indistinguishable from what
          your engineers would create manually.
      - q: What happens when our BIM software gets updated?
        a: >-
          We maintain compatibility with new software versions as part of our ongoing
          support. When Autodesk, Trimble, or Nemetschek release updates, we test and
          update the plugin to make sure it continues to work. You do not need to worry
          about an update breaking your automation.
      - q: Can the plugin handle project specific requirements?
        a: >-
          Yes. Plugins can be configured to adapt to different project types, clients,
          or regional standards. If your office works on residential and commercial
          projects with different requirements, the plugin can detect the project type
          and apply the right rules automatically. We build in flexibility from the
          start so you do not need a separate tool for every scenario.
      - q: Do we need programming knowledge to use the plugins?
        a: >-
          No. The plugins are designed for engineers and planners, not developers. Your
          team interacts with them through buttons, menus, and dialog boxes inside the
          BIM software they already know. No scripting, no command lines, no technical
          setup. If someone can use Revit or Tekla, they can use the plugin.
