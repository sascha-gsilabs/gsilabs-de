  - type: faq
    title: Questions developers ask us
    note: If you have further questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
    cta: { label: Contact us, href: /get-started }
    items:
      - q: What data does the system need to get started?
        a: >-
          At minimum, historic cost data from your completed projects in any structured
          format. Spreadsheets, ERP exports, or even scanned final cost reports. The more
          projects you provide, the more accurate the model becomes. We also integrate
          publicly available market data and cost indices automatically.
      - q: How accurate are the estimates?
        a: >-
          Accuracy depends on the quality and quantity of your historic data. With a
          portfolio of 10 or more comparable completed projects, the system typically
          achieves estimates within 5% to 10% of actual final costs at feasibility stage.
          This improves over time as more projects are completed and fed back into the
          model.
      - q: Can this replace our cost consultants?
        a: >-
          It is designed to complement your cost consultants, not replace them. The system
          handles the data driven baseline estimate so your consultants can focus on the
          elements that require judgment, market knowledge, and negotiation. Many of our
          clients use the system to generate a first estimate in minutes, then have their
          consultants refine it.
      - q: Does it work for different development types?
        a: >-
          Yes. The system supports residential, commercial, mixed use, and industrial
          development. Each type uses its own cost model based on relevant benchmarks. If
          you develop across multiple types, the system maintains separate models and
          applies the right one automatically based on project parameters.
      - q: How does it handle market price changes?
        a: >-
          The system updates its material and labor cost assumptions regularly based on
          market index data. When you run an estimate, it uses the most current pricing
          available. For projects with long planning horizons, the system can model cost
          escalation scenarios based on historic price trends.
      - q: Can we use this for projects in different regions?
        a: >-
          Yes. The system supports regional cost models that reflect local labor rates,
          material availability, regulatory costs, and market conditions. We currently
          cover the German market in detail, with other European markets being added. Each
          region uses its own set of benchmarks and cost drivers.
