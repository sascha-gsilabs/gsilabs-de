As a Software Project Manager, you are the person who makes sure our client projects
move forward on time, on scope, and at the quality level our clients expect.

#### What we are looking for

You will manage the delivery of AI and BIM automation projects for AEC companies across
the globe. That means coordinating between our engineering team and the client,
translating technical requirements into clear milestones, and keeping everyone aligned
when priorities shift. You are comfortable talking to a structural engineer about
reinforcement logic in the morning and reviewing a sprint board with developers in the
afternoon.

#### The right candidate will have

- Experience managing software development projects, ideally in a consulting or client facing environment
- Understanding of AEC workflows or a strong willingness to learn how construction, engineering, and manufacturing companies operate
- Ability to break down complex technical deliverables into structured project plans with clear milestones
- Strong communication skills in English and German, both written and verbal
- Experience with agile delivery methods and tools like Jira, Linear, or similar
- Comfort working directly with clients, running status meetings, and managing expectations

#### Work at GSI Labs

We are a small team that builds real solutions for real clients. No proof of concepts
that sit on a shelf. Every project you manage will be used in production by engineers,
planners, and manufacturers. You will have direct access to leadership, full visibility
into every project, and the freedom to shape how we deliver.

> We build technology for the people who design, build, and maintain the built
> environment. The Project Manager is the person who makes sure it actually reaches
> them.

If you want to manage projects that matter for an industry that affects how billions of
people live and work, get in touch.
