  - type: pillars
    eyebrow: Why it matters
    title: >-
      Most AI tools ask you to send your project data somewhere else. For a lot of firms
      that is where the conversation ends.
    lede: >-
      Contracts, correspondence, drawings and cost data are the most sensitive material a
      construction business holds. Client agreements and public sector work often rule out
      sending any of it to a service you do not control. We build the systems so that
      question does not come up: the model runs where your data already lives.
    items:
      - title: Deployment on Your Terms
        body: >-
          On premise, in your own cloud tenancy, or in an EU region you nominate. The
          decision is yours and it can change later without rebuilding the system.
      - title: Nothing Leaves the Perimeter
        body: >-
          Data is processed in encrypted environments and is never passed to third
          parties or used to train anyone else's model. GDPR compliance is the starting
          point, not an add on.
      - title: Built Into What You Run
        body: >-
          It connects to the systems your teams already use, Microsoft 365, Google
          Workspace, your document management, without migration and without asking
          anyone to change how they work.
    note: >-
      * What a deployment looks like depends on your existing infrastructure, your
      security requirements, and the data you want the system to reach.

  - type: stages
    id: how
    title: How we build it
    items:
      - title: Map the Data and the Constraints
        body: >-
          We start with two questions: which data should the system be able to reach, and
          what are you not allowed to do with it. That means going through your document
          stores, your mail, your project systems, and through the client agreements and
          regulations that govern them. The output is a clear picture of what is in scope
          and what the deployment has to satisfy before anything is built.
      - title: Stand Up the Infrastructure
        body: >-
          We deploy the model and the retrieval layer into the environment you chose, and
          build the ingestion pipeline that keeps it current as projects move. Access
          follows the permissions you already have: someone who cannot open a document
          today cannot get an answer from it tomorrow. Everything is tested against your
          real data before anyone relies on it.
      - title: Hand Over and Keep It Running
        body: >-
          Your team gets the system, the documentation, and the training to operate it.
          We stay on for monitoring, updates and the new use cases that surface once
          people see what it can do. The point is that you own the result, including the
          option to run it without us.

  - type: statement
    title: Sovereignty is an architecture decision, not a policy document.
    body: >-
      Plenty of vendors will tell you their tool is GDPR compliant. Far fewer will let you
      see where the model runs, what leaves the building, and what happens to the system
      if you stop paying them. We build so that those answers are yours to give.
    points:
      - The model and the data sit in an environment you control and can audit
      - Access rights are inherited from your systems rather than managed separately
      - No lock in by design. You keep the deployment, the pipeline, and the documentation
