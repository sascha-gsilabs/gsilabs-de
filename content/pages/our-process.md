  - type: faq
    title: Frequently Asked Questions
    note: If you have further questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
    cta: { label: Contact us, href: /get-started }
    items:
      - q: How long does a typical engagement take from start to deployment?
        a: >-
          Most projects go from first workshop to a working solution in 8 to 12 weeks. The
          exact timeline depends on the complexity of the workflow, the number of systems
          involved, and how quickly your team can provide feedback during the validation
          phase. Simpler automations like report generation can be live in 4 to 6 weeks.
          Larger implementations with multiple integrations typically take 3 to 4 months.
      - q: Do we need to change our existing tools or systems?
        a: >-
          No. We build solutions that integrate with what you already use. Whether that is
          Revit, Tekla, Allplan, SAP, Microsoft 365, or custom internal tools, we connect to
          your existing infrastructure. The goal is to add intelligence to your current
          setup, not to replace it.
      - q: How do you ensure the AI output meets our quality standards?
        a: >-
          Every solution goes through a validation phase where your team reviews the output
          against real project data. We do not deploy anything without sign off from
          qualified people on your side. After deployment, automated verification layers
          continue to check results against plausibility ranges, code limits, and historical
          benchmarks. If something looks wrong, it gets flagged for human review.
      - q: What happens after the initial solution is deployed?
        a: >-
          We stay involved. Every deployment includes a monitoring period where we track how
          the system performs in daily use. We fix issues, refine the logic based on real
          world feedback, and optimize performance over time. Most clients then expand to
          additional use cases once the first solution is running smoothly.
      - q: How is pricing structured?
        a: >-
          We work on a project basis with clear scope and deliverables. There is no hourly
          billing and no open ended retainers. You know what you are paying for before we
          start. For ongoing support and expansion after the initial project, we offer
          maintenance agreements tailored to your usage and needs.
      - q: Can we start with a small pilot before committing to a larger project?
        a: >-
          Yes, and we encourage it. Most engagements begin with a single workflow or use
          case. This lets you see measurable results quickly and build confidence before
          scaling. The pilot is a full production solution, not a demo or proof of concept.
          If it works, we expand. If it does not, you have not committed to anything beyond
          the pilot scope.
