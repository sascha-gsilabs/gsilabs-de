      Last updated August 2026.

      GSI Software GmbH ("GSI Labs", "we", "us") takes the protection of your personal
      data seriously. This privacy policy explains what data we collect when you visit
      our website, how we use it, and what rights you have. The responsible party is GSI
      Software GmbH, Rudi-Dutschke-Str. 23, 10969 Berlin, Germany. Email:
      info@gsilabs.de. Managing Director: Sascha Avermiddig.

      #### Hosting and General Data Collection

      This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723,
      USA, which acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR. When you open a page, Vercel
      records server log data including your IP address, browser type, operating system,
      referring URL, time of access, and the pages visited. This is processed on the
      basis of our legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website
      (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

      Vercel serves the site from a worldwide network of servers, and data may be
      transferred to the United States in the process. That transfer is covered by the
      European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR, which
      form part of the data processing agreement we have concluded with Vercel.

      #### Visitor Recognition and Your Consent

      Beyond what is technically necessary to deliver the site, this website uses one
      further service, described just below. It stores information in your browser and
      reads it again later, and it runs only if you have agreed to it. We ask when you
      first open the site, and until you agree nothing beyond the technically necessary
      is loaded. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section
      25(1) TDDDG.

      You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, through the
      tracking settings linked in the footer of every page. Withdrawing is as easy as
      giving consent and does not affect the lawfulness of anything processed before.
      If you decline, the website works exactly as it does otherwise.

      With your consent we use the visitor recognition service of ZenLeads Inc.,
      trading as Apollo.io, 440 N Barranca Ave #4750, Covina, CA 91723-1722, USA. It
      receives your IP address, the pages you open, the
      page that referred you and technical details of your browser, and sends them to
      assets.apollo.io and aplo-evnt.com. It sets no cookies. Instead it keeps an
      identifier called apolloAnonId, a random value with no name attached to it, in
      your browser's local storage, where it stays until you clear that storage or
      withdraw your consent, which deletes it. Apollo.io compares the IP address with
      its own database of company networks. The purpose is to learn which companies
      take an interest in our services, so that we can approach them. What comes out is
      the name of an organisation rather than your name, but your IP address is
      personal data and is processed to get there. Where no company can be matched,
      nothing is recorded.

      Apollo's terms of service also allow the company to use the data we send it for
      its own ends: to grow, enrich and verify its own database of business contacts,
      and, once aggregated or stripped of identifiers, to improve its products. For
      that use Apollo decides the purposes itself and is not acting on our
      instructions.

      Apollo.io processes this data in the United States. That transfer is covered by
      the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR,
      which form part of the data processing addendum that Apollo's terms of service
      incorporate. Those clauses do not put the data out of reach of US authorities,
      and you should know that before you agree.

      #### Nothing beyond the necessary loads until you agree, and you can withdraw that agreement from any page.

      #### Contact, External Services, and Your Rights

      If you contact us through one of the forms on this website or by email, we store the
      information you provide (name, email address, message content, and for applications
      the details you enter in the application form) to process your inquiry. The legal
      basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

      The forms on this website are provided by HubSpot Ireland Limited, which processes
      the data you submit on our behalf as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The form is
      served from HubSpot's EU infrastructure, so your entries are stored on servers in the
      European Union. Loading a page that carries a form causes your browser to contact
      HubSpot, which receives your IP address for that request.

      The typefaces used on this website are hosted on our own servers. No font request
      is made to Google or any other third party.

      Under the GDPR, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16),
      erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20),
      and the right to object (Art. 21). Where processing rests on your consent, you may
      withdraw it at any time (Art. 7(3)). You may also lodge a complaint with the
      Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59-61,
      10555 Berlin.
